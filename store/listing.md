## Full description (EN)
GORE ZONE is a 3D ragdoll physics sandbox for adults.

• 30+ weapons and tools: from fists, a shovel and a katana to shotguns, a crossbow, a flamethrower, a taser, a gravity gun and an RPG
• Real ragdoll physics: bodies fall, tumble, crawl when wounded and fly apart
• Location-based wounds: shot legs, belly wounds, throats; bandages and medkits help
• Dismemberment: sever limbs, blow up heads, blades and arrows stay stuck in bodies, blood stays on floors, walls and clothes
• A living world: people chat, make calls, smoke, hide behind cars, surrender on their knees and react to death
• Police (good and bad cops), gangs with a boss, medics, firefighters, dogs and zombies
• Cars: get behind the wheel, run people over, crash and blow them up
• Reality Crusher: spawn people, cars and props, grab, throw, rip off limbs, push, revive and delete
• Modes: sandbox, survival, zombie survival and timed BHOP courses
• Slow-mo, flight, day/night cycle, realistic death — all in the settings
• 6 maps, 4 languages (English UK/US, Russian, Ukrainian)
• Fully offline, no ads, no purchases, no sign-up

Warning: contains intense violence and blood. 18+ only.
